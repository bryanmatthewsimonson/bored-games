# Original Current Affairs artwork

The map of the Commonwealth (its coastline, regions, the Long Water, the Copper Range, the lines and the city medallions), the 42 power-station cards and the Era III card, the fuel tokens, the fuel yard, the money glyph, the seat tokens and marks, the cover and the thumbnail (in `apps/web/src/games/current-affairs/`, with the cover and thumbnail exported to `docs/games/current-affairs/art/`) were created for this repository. No artwork, board drawing, card layout or trade dress from a published game or gaming site is included.
