# Original Sticks & Stones artwork

The clan camp board, the discovery cards and their symbols, the monument tiles, the goods, tools and people tokens, the dice, the cover and the thumbnail (in `apps/web/src/games/sticks-and-stones/`) were created for this repository. No artwork, board drawing or layout from a published game or gaming site is included.
