# Original Right of Way artwork

The board, freight cards, charters and cover SVGs in `docs/games/right-of-way/art/`, and the interactive SVG artwork and associated visual design in `apps/web/src/games/right-of-way/`, were created originally for this implementation. No artwork from a published game or gaming site is included.

These original visual assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . This dedication concerns these original visual assets, not trademarks or rights held by third parties.
