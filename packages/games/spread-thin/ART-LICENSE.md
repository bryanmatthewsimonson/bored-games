# Original Spread Thin artwork

The chart of the Wide World (its sea, shelves and coastlines, the routes and the city markers), the four strain marks and their colours, the case tokens, the clinic, the pawns, the seven role badges, the city, relief and surge cards, the cover and the thumbnail (in `apps/web/src/games/spread-thin/`, with the cover and thumbnail exported to `docs/games/spread-thin/art/`) were created for this repository. No artwork, board drawing, card layout or trade dress from a published game or gaming site is included.
