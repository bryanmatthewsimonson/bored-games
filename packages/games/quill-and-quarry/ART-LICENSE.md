# Quill & Quarry artwork

The quill, cut-stone emblem, engraved landscape, letter-tile treatment, board frame, premium palette and layouts were created originally for this repository. Vector source is in `apps/web/src/games/quill-and-quarry/art.tsx`; the interactive board and styles sit alongside it. No external images, proprietary fonts, reference-game logos or copied packaging are included. The art is available under the same terms as this repository. The title is a working name, not a representation of trademark registration or clearance.
